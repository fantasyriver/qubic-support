Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

A game on your device

Qubic is a local two-player board game. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics service, tracking, chat, or online multiplayer. We do not collect or receive your gameplay, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or payment details through the app.

What stays in the app

The app saves the number of completed 4×4×4 games and your unlocked characters, sound settings, and language choice in its local storage. An active board and the X/O session score stay in memory; they reset when the app process ends. This information is used only to run the game and is not sent to the developer.

Retention and deletion

Unlock progress and settings remain until the app's data is removed. A normal update of the same app preserves them. Uninstalling or clearing the app's data generally removes local progress. Qubic does not provide its own cloud sync. Device backups, restores, and app offloading are controlled by iPadOS and may preserve or restore app data according to your system settings.

Purchases and other services

App Store purchases and payment processing are handled by Apple under Apple's policies. Qubic has no in-app purchases or subscriptions and does not store payment information. Packaged game assets and sounds load locally; no external resource download is needed to play.

If you contact us

If you choose to contact support, we receive only the information you send, such as your email address, message, and attachments. It is used to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment details. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence; records needed to resolve a dispute or meet legal obligations may be retained.

About this website

The companion privacy and support pages do not use advertising or analytics scripts. Their hosting provider may process normal web requests, including IP addresses and connection metadata, to serve and protect the site under that provider's policy. Visiting the website is separate from playing Qubic offline.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the current policy. The latest policy is available through the Privacy policy button in the app.
