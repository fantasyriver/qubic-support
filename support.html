How to play

  1. Share one iPad with another person; Qubic has no AI or online opponent.
  2. Choose 3×3×3 to connect three, or 4×4×4 to connect four. Gravity starts on; turn it off before playing for free placement.
  3. X goes first. Select a column or cell and confirm each move. Rotate the board or use the labeled grid.
  4. Complete classic games to unlock friends at 3, 10, 20, 35, and 50 games. Wins and draws count; abandoned and easy games do not.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play offline?

Yes. The installed app includes the models, backgrounds, fonts, and sounds needed to play. No account is required.

Where is my progress?

Character unlocks and preferences stay in the app’s local storage. Normal updates preserve them. Uninstalling or clearing data can remove them; Qubic has no cloud sync.

Why did my score disappear?

The X/O score belongs to the current app process. It resets when you reset it or the process ends. Unlock progress is separate.

Why is there no Chinese switch?

The interface starts in English. Chinese/English switching is available only when your primary system language is Simplified Chinese.

Why is there no sound?

Tap a game control to start audio, check the in-app speaker panel, and check your iPad volume. Music, ambience, and effects have separate controls.

What should I include in a support request?

Describe the issue, app version, iPad model, and iPadOS version. Attach a screenshot if useful. Do not include passwords, payment information, or unrelated personal data.
